# Motion capture credits — CharacterLab

## CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database

The `.bvh` clips in this folder are derived from the Carnegie Mellon University
Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database:

- http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
- BVH conversions redistributed via https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap

**Attribution (required by CMU):**

> The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
> The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

You may use this data in research and commercial projects, but you may **not**
resell the motion data itself (even in converted form).
