K9 DOG — Howitzer defence simulation game
=========================================

Educational sandbox. Fiction. Not affiliated with any manufacturer, armed force, or government.

DISCLAIMER
----------
"K9 DOG" is a cheeky lab name for a fictional educational 155 mm L52-class howitzer.
It is not a manufacturer product and not an official training or certification system.

Ballistic probable-error budgets, muzzle velocities, coastal met clocks, and warhead
effect radii are open educational approximations for gameplay and agent labs — not
firing tables, not METAR products, and not classified or controlled technical data.

PRODUCT
-------
Title: K9 DOG — Howitzer defence simulation game
Host: Novolis Silk GAME lab (ArtillerySimulator)
Controls: WASD lay, Space fire, F shell-cam, Esc/P pause, L licenses

LEVELS
------
Maps/scenarios are pluggable (GameLevelCatalog / IGameLevel).
Shipped level: Defense of Kristiansand (kristiansand.defense) on Odderøya.
Scenarios are fictional defense exercises, not targeting instruction.
CLI: --list-levels / --level <id> / --seed <int|hex>

MAP / GEO DATA
--------------
Height and road layers may derive from public Kartverket sources (e.g. NHM DTM)
and NVDB vegnett extracts. Follow those providers' license and attribution
requirements when redistributing map binaries or rebuild scripts.
Rebuild tooling lives under scripts/ (not part of the shipped game binary).
