AISI 316L t = 8 mm flat only — no bend stock 2×6 m skin ≈ 242.948 t
Process lock: Outer hull is a faceted flat-plate weldment (pepakura). Plates are shear/plasma/laser cut from annealed sheet and joined — not brake-formed, not rolled, not stretch-formed. OML = exterior; IML = interior of skin (OML inset by t = 8 mm).

1 · Material selection (space + flat sheet)

Selected: AISI 316L

  • UNS S31603 · EN 1.4404 / X2CrNiMo17-12-2
  • Product: Annealed flat plate / sheet (cut to facet nets)
  • Density ρ = 8000 kg/m³
  • E ≈ 193 GPa · ν ≈ 0.29
  • Sy ≈ 170 MPa · Su ≈ 485 MPa (annealed typical — use mill cert)
  • CTE ≈ 16×10⁻⁶ /K

Why this alloy

  • L-grade: as-welded construction without sensitization PWHT (ECSS guidance for low-C austenitics).
  • Flat sheet: 301 is for cold-worked formed sheet; this hull is explicitly not bent
  • Space listing: AISI 316L — space experience: Good (SPACEMATDB).
  • 316L vs 304L: Both L-grades weld as-welded; 316L Mo improves ground-assembly / hangar pitting margin and is the usual structural plate pick in SPACEMATDB-class lists
  • Not carbon steel: Carbon/HSLA needs coatings and has poor vacuum/outgas + corrosion story for long dwell
  • Not Ti for this build: Ti-6Al-4V better specific strength but costly/slow for large flat pepakura sheet weldments
Vacuum notes: No atmospheric corrosion in vacuum; oxide does not self-heal if abraded Risk of cold welding metal-to-metal in vacuum — separate faying surfaces / coatings at mechanisms Filler: ER316L / ISO 14343 19 12 3 L · as-welded (L-grade; no PWHT required for sensitization control). Qualify to GTAW / GMAW per ECSS-Q-ST-70-39 class welding (or NASA-STD-5006 analogue).
